BEHIND THE DOUGH

Why tangzhong?

Hot-water starch gelatinization holds on to extra moisture, contributing to a softer, longer-lasting crumb. Poolish adds aroma and flavour through prefermentation. Pizza mode adapts the shaping and bake to that same flour–water architecture.
